# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be safe, constructive, and welcoming.
Treat other contributors with respect, assume good intent, give actionable
feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing
someone else's private information, or encouraging any of these behaviors.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, and other project
spaces. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact listed on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/XiyaoWang0519). Do not use a public issue
for sensitive reports. Include relevant links or screenshots when possible.
Do not include real credentials, phone numbers, or call transcripts.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith, protect reporter privacy as
far as practical, and may edit or remove content, warn participants, limit
participation, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces. Knowingly
false or bad-faith reports may receive the same response.
